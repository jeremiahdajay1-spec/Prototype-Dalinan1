Our Sustainability Mission

Committed to revolutionizing the laundry industry with eco-friendly solutions

From Plastic Waste to Paper Promise

Dalinan was founded in 2020 by laundry industry veterans who witnessed the massive plastic waste generated by commercial detergents. We set out to create a powerful cleaning solution that doesn't compromise on performance while eliminating plastic packaging.

Our innovative paper packaging technology maintains product integrity while being fully recyclable and compostable. Each container saves approximately 3 plastic jugs from entering landfills or oceans.
